## Description (1,804 of 4,000)

```
You're in Portugal. Get to Poland. Which countries are in the way?

Border Hopper is a geography puzzle about the world's borders. You're given a start and a destination, and you travel between them by naming each country in between, one border at a time. Your little explorer hops across every border as you go. Find the shortest route for a perfect score.

EVERY BORDER IS REAL
All 195 countries, drawn from real map data. If two countries share a land border, you can cross it. You'll be surprised which countries touch, and which ones don't.

WAYS TO PLAY
• Campaign: 1,000 levels, from quick two-step trips to routes that cross whole continents.
• Daily challenge: one new route every day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going.
• Random: pick Easy, Medium or Hard and play as many as you like.
• Flight mode: every country has a departures board. Hop across real sea crossings like Dover to Calais, or take a long-haul flight to the other side of the world. Island nations like Japan and Australia join the game here.

FILL YOUR PASSPORT
Every country you travel through earns a stamp in your passport. Collect stamps across six continents, and dress your explorer in the flag of any country you've visited.

EXPLORE THE MAP
Zoom right out to see the whole world, or pinch in on the tiny countries. The map wraps all the way round the globe, so you can keep going east and come back from the west.

MADE TO BE PLAYED ANYWHERE
• No account needed. Your progress is saved on your device.
• Quick rounds that fit a coffee break.
• A gentle hop at every border and a jingle when you arrive, with sound and haptics you can switch off.
• Reduce motion option for a calmer map.
• Share your route with friends after every game.

Learn the world one border at a time, without it ever feeling like homework.
```
